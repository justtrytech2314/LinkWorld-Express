Frequently Asked Questions

Need Help?

Find answers to the most common questions about shipping, tracking and delivery.

How long does international shipping take?

Delivery times vary depending on destination and service selected. Express shipments usually arrive within 2–5 business days while Standard delivery may take 5–10 business days.

Can I track my shipment?

Yes. Every shipment receives a unique tracking number allowing real-time tracking from pickup until final delivery.

Are shipments insured?

Optional shipment insurance is available for valuable cargo, documents and commercial goods.

How do I contact support?

Our logistics specialists are available 24 hours a day via telephone and email for shipment assistance.
