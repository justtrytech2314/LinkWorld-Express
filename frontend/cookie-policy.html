Privacy Policy Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function correctly, remember your preferences, and — where enabled — understand how visitors use the site.

How We Ask For Consent

When you first visit LinkWorld Express, a cookie banner lets you Accept All, allow Essential Only, or Manage Preferences to choose exactly which categories below you're comfortable with. Your choice is remembered for 180 days.

Categories Of Cookies We Use

Category Purpose Can Be Disabled?
Essential Required for core site functionality — page navigation, admin authentication, and security. The site cannot function correctly without these. No
Analytics Would help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. No analytics service is currently active on this site. Yes
Preference Remembers settings you've chosen, such as your cookie consent preferences, to provide a more convenient return visit. Yes
Marketing Would support relevant promotional content if enabled in the future. No marketing or advertising cookies are currently active on this site. Yes

We only activate a cookie category once you've consented to it — nothing beyond Essential cookies runs before you make a choice.

Managing Your Preferences

You can change your cookie choices at any time by clearing this site's cookies and local storage in your browser, which will show the consent banner again on your next visit.

Third-Party Cookies

Some pages load resources from third parties (such as web fonts and map tiles) that may set their own cookies under their respective privacy policies. LinkWorld Express does not control these third-party cookies.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our website evolves. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
